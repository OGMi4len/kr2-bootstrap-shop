A GUIDE FOR OWNERS

Prepare without rushing

Choosing care and using the demo booking form.

First visit

Start with the catalogue. Each service lists suitable pets, care steps and duration. Use the comparison page if you are unsure.

Care for cats

Choose the Cats filter. Bath and brush, hygiene care, nail trim and de-shedding are available in this example catalogue. Card illustrations do not restrict the choice.

Choosing a time

Choose a date up to 90 days ahead. Example appointments start every half hour from 10:00 and must finish by 19:00. For today, only future start times are offered. Real availability is not checked.

How pricing works

The catalogue shows a starting price per visit. The calculator multiplies it by 1–12 identical visits. The booking form covers one visit without taking payment.

After your visit

Ask the groomer what was included, what they noticed and how to continue care at home. The home page demonstrates this sequence.

What happens to your booking

The form checks the fields in your browser and shows a summary. There is no server, transmission, payment or reservation. Details are not stored in localStorage and disappear when the page closes.

Before-visit checklist 

Before visiting HvostKom
□ Choose a service suitable for your pet.
□ Check duration and estimated price.
□ Bring your pet’s usual carrier or lead.
□ Note any behaviour and care needs.
□ Confirm the care steps with the groomer.
